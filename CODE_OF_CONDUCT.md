# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that makes this project better:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback on a change.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a contribution, and asking why rather
  than assuming malice.
- Being clear about uncertainty. "I think this leaks, here is why" is more useful
  than either false confidence or silence.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project uses — issues, pull requests, commit
messages, discussions — and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/rmonteiro-pereira/energy-forecast-drift/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repository has. Reports are handled by the
maintainer, who will keep the reporter's identity confidential.

Consequences scale with the behaviour: a private word for a first lapse, a
warning, and removal from the project's spaces for anything sustained or severe.
